# Benchmark results

Generated by `kites-bench` from the programs in `benchmarks/`. Do not edit by hand; re-run the tool instead.

Cycles measured on the Kites simulator. **Original** is the program as written (on NH_NF and NH_F padded with just the NOPs it needs to be correct). **Reordered** is the optimizer's suggestion. **+ unrolled** also unrolls counted loops by up to x4, keeping every register's final value. **+ folded** also lets registers that nothing reads after a loop end differently, so indexed loops step a pointer and the unrolled copies share one address computation (`--only-live-registers`). Every version was checked to compute exactly what the original computes; registers that only the optimizer uses (copies, pointers), and for **+ folded** the registers nothing reads after their loop, are not compared.
